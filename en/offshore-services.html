What are they?

What are offshore services?

Offshore services are the inspection, maintenance and construction jobs carried out at sea or over water: on platforms, subsea pipelines, vessels and quays. They include commercial diving, underwater inspection, corrosion protection, onboard tank cleaning and marine works. In Mexico, commercial diving is regulated by standard NOM-014-STPS-2000.

Compliance

What NOM-014 requires from your diving contractor

Before authorizing a dive on your asset, check that the contractor meets these points of Mexican standard NOM-014-STPS-2000.

Crew

With surface-supplied air: one supervisor and at least two divers down to 24 m (79 ft), adding divers as depth increases.

Standby diver

Always present and ready to intervene. Daily log with divers, depth and bottom time.

Chamber

Decompression chamber on site for dives deeper than 30 m (98 ft) or requiring decompression.

Limits

Scuba to 30 m (98 ft); surface-supplied to 50 m (164 ft). No diving with waves of 150 cm (about 5 ft) or more.
